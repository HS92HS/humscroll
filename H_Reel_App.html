Ways to scroll

Turn on any combination. Each one works on its own, and they can all be on at the same time.

Finger swipeSwipe up or down with a clean finger, like any reel app.
Messy handsSwipe with messy hands. After each swipe, a box asks you to wash for 8 seconds.
Step humA short hum moves one reel. Higher goes up, lower goes down.
Proportional humHold a hum. Higher or lower sets the direction, and the further from your normal note, the faster it moves.

When step and proportional hum are both on, a short hum steps and a longer hold moves continuously.
