How do you want to scroll?

Four ways to move through the feed. You can switch any time from the button at the top of the feed.

Videos
The feed has built-in animated clips. You can also load real videos from your phone. Videos always play muted, because their sound would confuse the hum detector.

Voice setup

Stay quiet

Quiet room. Phone about 20 cm from your mouth. Hum with lips closed: "mmmm".

We measure the background noise first.

Voice check

Try your voice

Normal note: the dot stays in the middle. Higher: it rises and glows blue. Lower: it drops and glows purple.

Your task

A video number appears at the top, for example "Go to video #57". Move through the feed to that video (its number glows orange) and stay on it for a moment. Then the next one appears. Be quick but careful.

You start with 3 practice rounds.

Messy hands test

How long does cleaning your hands take?

When you cook, your hands are often messy. Before you can touch the phone, you have to clean them first. We measure how long that takes for you.

  1. Put a little flour (or hand lotion) on your fingers.
  2. The researcher taps Start.
  3. Wipe your hands clean on the towel, then tap anywhere on the screen with a clean finger.
